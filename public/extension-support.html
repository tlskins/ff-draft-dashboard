Drafty Draft Sync Support

Drafty Draft Sync relays picks from supported fantasy draft rooms to an open Drafty dashboard.

Before reporting a capture problem

  1. Open the Drafty dashboard and the supported draft room in the same Chrome profile.
  2. Confirm Drafty reports that the draft source is connected.
  3. Reload both tabs after installing or updating the extension.
  4. Open the extension popup and confirm the installed version.

For bugs or support, open a Drafty support issue. Do not include private account information, authentication tokens, full browser-storage exports, or league credentials.

For a private account-data or deletion request, open an issue without personal details and ask for a private follow-up channel.

Read the Drafty Draft Sync privacy policy.
